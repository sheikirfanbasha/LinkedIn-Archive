Personal AI agents are silently slipping into our daily lives.

I have started exploring this firsthand by creating a new Google account specifically to experience Personal Intelligence in Gemini. I have enabled it and started forwarding selected emails from my personal account to see how Gemini uses that context to help me.

Personal AI tools like Meta Muse, OpenAI dots, and Google Gemini Spark are emerging, and they point to a fundamental shift: we need to build apps for agents, not just for users.

Are we ready to build apps for a world where AI agents, rather than users, initiate the interaction?
